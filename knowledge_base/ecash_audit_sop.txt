eCash Audit SOP

1. SDS and CMP eCash data must be reconciled by slot_location and gamingdt.

2. If SDS and CMP values match, the record is considered reconciled.

3. If eCash In or eCash Out variance is detected, the variance must be reviewed by a revenue audit user.

4. The auditor must download and review the reconciliation report before approving any adjustment.

5. CMP adjustments may be approved only when SDS is validated as the source of truth and the CMP variance is confirmed as a posting delay, missing update, or system synchronization issue.

6. All approved adjustments must include an adjustment reason, validation status, committed user, and timestamp.

7. If the adjusted CMP values do not match SDS values, the adjustment must not be committed.

8. If variance remains after adjustment, the issue must be escalated to Finance Systems Support or Revenue Audit Management.

9. The LLM should not calculate financial totals independently. Reconciliation calculations must be performed by the deterministic reconciliation tool.

10. The LLM may provide audit recommendations only using reconciliation output and SOP guidance.
